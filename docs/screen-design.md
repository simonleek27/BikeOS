# Color screen design (ILI9341, 320×240)

Agreed with ND on 2026-11-19. This replaces the 128×64 monochrome layout on the
2.8-inch ILI9341 TFT. The ST7565 path keeps the old renderer.

## Layout

Landscape, 320×240, map first.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
│╭──────────────╮         ╭ 14:32  ●  ▯ ╮ │
││ ↱   120 m    │         ╰─────────────╯ │
││ Rozengracht  │            ╭ ETA 14:48 ╮│
││ then left 300│            ╰───────────╯│
│╰──────────────╯                          │
│                 MAP                      │
│                  ▲  rider, lower third   │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Map** fills the whole screen edge to edge, heading-up. The rider arrow
  sits in the lower third (y ≈ 172) so more road ahead is visible.
- **Turn card** floats top-left, about 150×80 px with 10 px rounded corners:
  turn arrow on the left, large distance, the street being turned onto, and a
  "then left in 120 m" line when the following maneuver is within 250 m
  (the card grows to fit that line).
- **Status chips** float top-right: clock with a GPS dot and phone glyph, and
  under it an ETA chip while navigating.

## Map

| Layer | Day | Night |
|---|---|---|
| Land | warm off-white | charcoal |
| Water / canals | light blue fill | deep blue fill |
| Parks | light green fill | dark green fill |
| Residential streets | white | dark gray |
| Major streets | pale amber, wider | lighter gray, wider |
| Bike paths | solid teal | solid teal |
| Route ahead | magenta, thick | magenta, thick |
| Route already ridden | gray | gray |

- Street labels: major streets and named canals, horizontal with a halo,
  never under the card or chips, never overlapping each other.
- Zoom: fixed while cruising (~1.6 m/px, about 500 m across). In the last
  ~80 m before a turn it eases in to ~0.9 m/px, and back out after.

## Themes

Automatic day/night from the GPS clock and position: night when the sun is
more than 2° below the horizon. Before the GPS has a time fix the screen uses
the night theme, which reads well in both conditions on a TFT.

## Behavior

The card stays calm: no growing or flashing as a turn approaches; the distance
counts down. Problem states tint the card but keep the layout.

| State | Card |
|---|---|
| Navigating | Neutral card, turn arrow, distance, street |
| Phone disconnected | Same as navigating; phone glyph goes gray |
| Off route / rerouting | Amber card, arrow grayed, "Off route" / "Rerouting" |
| GPS lost | Red card, "GPS lost", "Showing last position" |
| Waiting for GPS | Neutral card, "Waiting for GPS" |
| No route | Neutral card, "Pick a destination" / "on your phone"; map follows the rider |
| Arrived | Checkered flag, "Arrived", destination street |

Chips: the clock shows `--:--` until the GPS has given a time. Local time is
Europe/Amsterdam (CET/CEST). ETA assumes 15 km/h over the remaining distance.
The GPS dot is green with a fix, red when lost, gray otherwise; the phone
glyph is blue/green when connected and orange when disconnected.

The current color renderer labels both indicators in the status chip (`GPS` and
`BT`) so a valid GPS fix cannot be mistaken for a phone connection. The BT label
uses `NavigationRuntime::set_phone_connected()` and turns off immediately on the
BLE disconnect callback.

## Implementation notes

- `core/include/bikeos/canvas.hpp`: RGB565 canvas (153,600 bytes, PSRAM on the
  ESP32-S3) with polygon, thick-line, rounded-rect and anti-aliased text drawing.
- `core/include/bikeos/fonts.hpp`: DejaVu Sans bitmap fonts with 4-bit alpha,
  generated by `tools/generate_fonts.py` (Bitstream Vera / DejaVu license).
- `core/include/bikeos/color_renderer.hpp`: the layout above.
- Map format v4 adds water and park polygons (`AreaPolygon`) after the roads.
  v1–v3 files still decode with no areas.
- The TFT driver only resends row bands that changed since the last frame.
- `bikeos_color_preview` (host) writes PNGs of every state to check the design
  without hardware.
